# Policies and support

## Late work and extensions

The first assessment (brushwork study and attribution note) and the dossier accept a 48-hour extension on
request, no reason required, if you ask before the deadline rather than
after it. The final project does not: it is due in the same week as the
in-class defense, and a defense cannot be extended on its own.

## Seminar attendance

Seminars run on close reading of a single object --- a painting, a seal, a
colophon --- brought to the room. Missing more than two without warning
means you arrive at the dossier without having practised the argument style
it asks for, which shows in the mark far more reliably than the attendance
line on a spreadsheet does. Tell Vera in advance if you'll miss one; the
seminar plan usually survives.

## Declaring assistance

Every assessment in this course is, one way or another, an argument that a
specific claim is or isn't true. That makes the ordinary university rule ---
declare what you used and how --- unusually load-bearing here: an
attribution note that quietly took its central claim from a chatbot and
presented it as your own reading is exactly the failure mode the whole course
studies, just moved from a scroll to a paragraph. Declare any AI or outside
research assistance in a short footnote at the end of the piece it touched.
Undeclared assistance is treated as a misattribution, not a citation slip:
harshly, for reasons the course should have already made clear to you by
week 3.

## Painting submissions

The supplied landscape is an AI-generated teaching reference inspired by Tang
Yin; it is not a historical artwork. You may use it as the assigned model, but
must paint your own submissions. Do not trace it, apply a filter to it or submit
AI-generated painting as your own brushwork. Manual digital painting is welcome.
Keep process photographs, layers or time-lapses and credit all references.
Sign with your own name; do not add a simulated Tang Yin signature or seal.
If materials or a physical painting setup are a barrier, contact the teaching
team early to arrange a suitable way to demonstrate the same observation skills.

## Getting help

Office hours and seminar time cover almost everything. For anything that
can't wait, email the person whose page names the topic --- Boris for
materials and seals, Vera for everything else --- rather than the shared
course address, which nobody is rostered to check between seminars.
