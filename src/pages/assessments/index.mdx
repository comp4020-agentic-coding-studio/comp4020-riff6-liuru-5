Paint, investigate, then paint again. All students work from the same
Tang Yin–inspired landscape, using practical studies to sharpen their judgement
about copies and authorship. No previous ink-painting experience is required.

- **25% · Brushwork study and attribution note:** paint the reference and explain your decisions.
- **35% · The dossier:** research a disputed historical painting and compare its details with your study.
- **40% · Final project:** make a revised painting and defend the relationship between resemblance and authorship.
