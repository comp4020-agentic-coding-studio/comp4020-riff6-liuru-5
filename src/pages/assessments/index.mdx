Look closely, check the evidence, then try making the marks yourself.
Begin with six paintings: three historical works and three generated imitations.
No previous ink-painting experience is required for the final project.

- **25% · Spot the real painting:** identify the three real works and explain your evidence before revealing the sources.
- **35% · The dossier:** investigate a disputed historical painting and revisit your visual judgement.
- **40% · Final project:** paint a study and a revised response to the shared landscape reference, then defend your choices.
