Every earlier week taught one instrument in isolation. This week applies all
of them --- materials, brushwork, seals, colophons, market context --- to a
single painting the seminar shortlists from three genuinely disputed
candidates in week 7's sourcebook, then argues in full. The point isn't to
land on a tidy verdict; it's to watch what happens when the instruments
disagree with each other, which they will, because that disagreement is the
normal condition of this field and not a sign that something has gone wrong.

Where the toolkit weeks asked "what does this kind of evidence show," this
week asks the harder question the dossier will require answering: when a
seal argues for and the brushwork argues against, what actually decides it,
and how do you write that decision down so a reader can check your
reasoning rather than just trust your conclusion.

## Outline

- the shortlisted painting, chosen live from three candidates
- working every instrument against it in turn, keeping a shared evidence log
- what to do when two kinds of evidence disagree
- the dossier is due at the end of this week: this seminar is the closest
  rehearsal for it you get
