## Before the seminar

Read the linked excerpts: a Zhang Daqian interview passage, a museum's own
account of acquiring one of his forgeries, and a short scholarly piece
arguing about how to categorise his work.

## In the seminar

Debate, structured as two sides: forgery-as-scholarship against
forgery-as-fraud, using Zhang's own casework as the shared evidence base
rather than abstract principle. Both sides have to use the same facts;
the disagreement should be about what the facts mean, not what they are.

## Afterwards

You don't need to resolve the debate --- week 11 deliberately complicates
it further. You do need a position of your own, briefly stated, because the
final project's defense in week 12 can reasonably ask you where you land.
