## Before the seminar

Skim the market sourcebook (workshop records, sale catalogues, and
collector correspondence, provided as translated excerpts) and flag two or
three paintings that catch your attention as genuinely contested rather
than merely undocumented.

## In the seminar

As a whole group, work from everyone's flagged paintings down to a
shortlist of three: the ones with real, documented disagreement in the
literature, spread across different kinds of dispute (a materials question,
a stylistic one, a provenance one) rather than three versions of the same
argument.

## Afterwards

The shortlist decided here is what week 8 works from, and it's also where
the dossier's painting will usually come from. If you already know which of
the three you want to argue, start reading ahead.
