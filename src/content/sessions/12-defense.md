## Before the seminar

Bring your final painting, first study and draft illustrated commentary,
plus notes for a ten-minute defense. The finished portfolio is due Friday.

## In the seminar

Each student defends their painting and research for ten minutes: a two-minute
introduction to their revisions and attribution reasoning, then questions from the room
aimed at the weakest point in the argument, the same discipline practised
all semester. Once every defense is done, re-sort week 1's six
reproductions and compare against your original piles.

## Afterwards

Incorporate feedback and submit your finished portfolio by Friday.
The re-sort comparison from this seminar is
also the honest answer to the semester's opening question --- whether the
toolkit actually changed your judgement, or just gave you better words for
the judgement you already had.
