## Before the seminar

Read the short packet of translated colophons and catalogue entries for
your assigned painting, provided on the course drive. Don't look at a
reproduction of the painting itself yet.

## In the seminar

Split into two groups. Group one argues attribution from the documentary
packet alone --- colophons, seals recorded in catalogues, provenance ---
with no image. Group two sees the reproduction and argues from visual
style alone, with no documentary packet. Compare verdicts at the end: where
they agree is unremarkable; where they don't is this week's actual content.

## Afterwards

Write two sentences, no more: your verbal-method verdict, and your
visual-method verdict, for your own painting. You'll want both on hand for
the attribution note in week 5.
