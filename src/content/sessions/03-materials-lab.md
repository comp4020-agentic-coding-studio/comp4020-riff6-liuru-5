## Before the seminar

Read the linked short-form materials-analysis case report (on the course
drive). Bring one question about a claim in it you don't yet know how to
evaluate.

## In the seminar

Boris runs a small hands-on station: paper and silk samples of different
ages under magnification, and a walk-through of how a real fibre or pigment
report gets produced. The second half works through your assigned case
report line by line, separating what the underlying test actually measured
from what the report's prose claims it proves.

## Afterwards

Materials evidence is one of the two independent legs of the attribution
note (the other is brushwork, from week 4). If you're leaning on a
materials claim in your note, it should survive the same "what does the
data actually show" test you just ran on someone else's report.
