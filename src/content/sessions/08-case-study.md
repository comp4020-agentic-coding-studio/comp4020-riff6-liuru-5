## Before the seminar

Bring your dossier draft. This seminar is the last structured chance to
stress-test it before submission.

## In the seminar

The seminar group picks one painting from week 7's shortlist and works it
in full, instrument by instrument, keeping a shared evidence log on the
board. Where two instruments disagree, the group has to write down both
readings rather than quietly picking the more convenient one.

## Afterwards

The dossier is due at the end of this week. It doesn't have to use the
seminar's shared painting --- your own dossier can argue a different
disputed work entirely --- but it does have to show the same discipline:
every instrument you use, logged, including the ones that didn't support
your conclusion.
