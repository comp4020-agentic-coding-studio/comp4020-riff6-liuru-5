## Before the seminar

Nothing to prepare. Arriving with an untrained eye is the point of this
seminar; every other week assumes you've read ahead.

## In the seminar

Six high-resolution reproductions, unlabelled: three from paintings with
secure, uncontested attributions to Tang Yin, three from paintings currently
disputed in the literature. Working alone first, then in pairs, sort them
into "confident" and "not sure," and write down the one feature that drove
each call. Nobody's sort is scored against the literature; what matters is
whether you can name your own reasoning.

## Afterwards

Keep your sorted list and your notes. You'll re-sort the same six
reproductions in week 12, with everything the semester adds, and the seminar
opens with how much (or how little) has changed.
