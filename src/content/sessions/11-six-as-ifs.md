## Before the seminar

Read the gatha itself, in translation, plus the short biographical note on
when and why Tang Yin adopted the name Liuru.

## In the seminar

Slow, collective close reading: each of the six images in turn (dream,
illusion, bubble, shadow, dew, lightning), asking what specifically it adds
that the others don't, before asking what it would mean to take the whole
line seriously as a stance on authenticity rather than as pious decoration.

## Afterwards

Nothing formal is due. Informally: reread your own attribution note or
dossier and notice whether it assumed a fixed, discoverable "real" painter
underneath the evidence, or something closer to what this seminar just
read. Either is defensible; knowing which one you did is the point.
