## Before the seminar

Read the translated colophon chain for your assigned scroll (course
drive). Note, for each colophon, who wrote it, when, and what they claim.

## In the seminar

Working in small groups, build a shared timeline of your scroll's
provenance from the colophon chain alone, treating each colophon as a
claim to be weighed rather than a fact to be logged. Identify the weakest
link --- the claim resting on the least independent support --- and be
ready to defend which one that is.

## Afterwards

This is the last seminar before the break. Nothing is due, but the market
lecture in week 7 assumes you can already read a provenance chain this
critically; a colophon chain you took at face value here will show in
week 8's case study.
